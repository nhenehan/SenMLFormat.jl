SenMLFormat and rsenml: performance

Written by bench/run.sh; do not edit by hand.

SenMLFormat is compared with rsenml only. Both read the same JSON files (bench/data): the RFC 8428 section 5.1.3 example and the 200-record synthetic pack of rsenml's compare (resolved records: three numeric sensors and a door switch, 30 s apart). Both are timed the same way (median_ns of rsenml's compare/src/perf.rs): calls batched to at least 200 µs, the median of 51 batches after 20 warm-up calls. bench/run.sh runs both three times, taking turns, and keeps the fastest median of each cell. Decoders validate and encoders validate first, as decode and encode do. "decode+resolve" is JSON decode, then resolution with a given "now".

  • CPU: AMD Ryzen 7 7840U w/ Radeon 780M Graphics
  • Julia: 1.12.7 (default flags)
  • rsenml: v0.9.1, rustc 1.99.0 (b940084d7 2026-09-28), release profile with LTO, one codegen unit
