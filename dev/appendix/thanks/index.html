Thanks

SenML is a small standard that does one job well, and SenMLFormat would not exist without the people who wrote it and the work it stands on.

  • Cullen Jennings, Zach Shelby, Jari Arkko, Ari Keränen and Carsten Bormann wrote RFC 8428. Its examples, hex dumps and schema are the first tests SenMLFormat must pass.
  • Daniel Peintner wrote EXIficient, with Richard Kuntschke at Siemens. SenMLFormat's EXI bytes are checked against EXIficient's.
  • Toralf Wittner (minicbor), Yevhenii Reizner (xmlparser) and Gronis (nanojson) wrote the Rust parsers whose grammar rules SenMLFormat's own readers follow, so both libraries agree on every input.

Thank you.
